## 4. Key Churn Drivers & Strategic Interventions

TreeExplainer (SHAP) feature attributions consistently identify the following leading behavioral indicators:
1. **Overdue Ratio (`recency / gap_mean`):** When elapsed days since last order exceed 2.0× a customer's typical cadence, churn likelihood spikes sharply.
2. **Order Count Trend (`orders_90d / orders_prior_90d`):** A drop below 0.50 signals accelerating customer disengagement.
3. **Return Rate:** Elevated item return rates negatively impact loyalty and correlate with elevated churn probability.

### Recommended Retention Playbook

- **High-Risk Champions / Big Spenders:** Proactive outreach from dedicated account managers, VIP exclusive offers, and direct service satisfaction checks.
- **Medium-Risk Loyal Customers:** Automated re-engagement email campaigns triggered when overdue ratio exceeds 1.5×.
- **At-Risk & Hibernating:** Win-back incentive promotions and feedback surveys to address root dissatisfaction.

## 5. Methodological Notes & Integrity Guarantees

- **Leakage Prevention:** Features are strictly constrained to historical data (date ≤ cutoff). Churn outcome labels are evaluated strictly in the forward 90-day window.
- **Purged Temporal Split:** Training, validation, and holdout test sets are separated by 90-day buffers to prevent temporal data leakage.
- **Model Calibration:** Models are calibrated using isotonic regression or Platt scaling to minimize the Brier score, ensuring probabilities reflect true empirical frequencies.
- **Champion Gating:** Production champion models are required to beat baseline PR-AUC and naive base rate Brier score on out-of-time test holdouts.
